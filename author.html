Modernization by Default, Modernization by Design

Building organizations that can evolve continuously

This is a practical leadership book for technology executives and architects. It is about how organizations keep evolving under continuous change, by making everyday modernization the default, and using deliberate design only when the trajectory must be reset. I am writing it from years of sitting with leaders in public sector digital, financial services, telecoms, and enterprise technology, including work across African markets where the pace of change rarely waits for a programme plan.

Coming 2026. Currently in production.

Themes

What the book explores

  1. Why transformation programmes stall Why traditional multi-year “transformation programmes” fail when change is continuous.
  2. Default and design Modernization by Default vs Modernization by Design as the operating model.
  3. Leadership in an AI-accelerated era Leadership, architecture, and measurement when the rate of change keeps rising.
