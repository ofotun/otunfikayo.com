About

The person behind the work

UK-based technology leader. Architecture, AI, and commercial systems — written with the same care as they are designed.

Fikayo Otun works as a senior solutions architect and technology leader — helping organisations find clarity when constraints are real and the rate of change does not pause for a programme plan.

His focus sits at the intersection of enterprise architecture, practical AI systems, and the commercial shape of technology decisions. The through-line is judgment: what to change by default, what to redesign deliberately, and how leaders keep that distinction alive under pressure.

He is the author of Modernization by Default, Modernization by Design (MbDD), a leadership book currently in production for 2026.

This site is personal — the person, the thinking, and selective advisory lanes. For channels and how to reach him, use Linktree.
