The service

The Career Coaching Programme is a 4-week core coaching programme for a single fee. It includes:

  • 6 x 60-minute 1:1 video sessions across the 4 weeks.
  • A written profile review covering your CV, LinkedIn, portfolio or GitHub.
  • A personalised gap analysis and 90-day roadmap.
  • 2 mock interviews with written feedback.
  • Async support between sessions, with replies within 2 working days.
  • CV, outreach and STAR story bank templates.

The fee also includes free post-employment coaching and mentoring once you start a new role: 7 x 30-minute 1:1 video debriefs across your first 90 days, with weekly debriefs in month 1, fortnightly debriefs in month 2 and a final debrief in month 3.

Post-employment support starts if you start a new role within 6 months of finishing the core programme. It is included in the fee at no extra cost and is a free extra. It does not change your cancellation rights for the core programme.
