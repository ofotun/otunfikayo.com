Author · MbDD

Modernization by Default, Modernization by Design

How to build organisations that keep adapting

I’m writing Modernization by Default, Modernization by Design for technology leaders who are tired of treating change as a series of big programmes with neat start and finish dates. The book explores how organisations can keep improving as part of everyday work—and when they need to stop, rethink the direction and redesign deliberately. It draws on my experience across public-sector digital, financial services, telecoms and enterprise technology, including work connected to African markets.

In development, with publication planned for 2026.

Themes

What the book explores

  1. Why transformation programmes stall Why traditional multi-year “transformation programmes” fail when change is continuous.
  2. Default and design Modernisation by Default vs Modernisation by Design as the operating model.
  3. Leadership in an AI-accelerated era Leadership, architecture, and measurement when the rate of change keeps rising.
