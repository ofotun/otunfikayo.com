How it works

A six-stage method

Each stage has a clear purpose and something concrete you leave with — not vague “career chats”.

  1. 01

    Goal assessment

    Where you are, where you want to be, which roles are realistic targets, and what constraints (time, location, visa, salary) shape the plan.

    Deliverable: written goal brief and target-role shortlist.

  2. 02

    Profile review

    CV, LinkedIn, and GitHub or portfolio — read the way a hiring manager or recruiter would, then marked up with specific changes.

    Deliverable: written profile feedback with prioritised edits.

  3. 03

    Planning

    A personalised gap analysis and a ninety-day roadmap covering skills, certifications, projects, and networking — sequenced so you know what to do next week, not just someday.

    Deliverable: gap analysis and 90-day roadmap document.

  4. 04

    Application coaching

    Targeting the right roles, tailoring CV and cover letter per application, and structuring recruiter outreach so you sound like a peer, not a blast email.

    Deliverable: tailored application pack and outreach templates.

  5. 05

    Interview coaching

    Mock interviews — behavioural, technical, and system design or architecture where the role needs it — with written feedback and pointers on salary and offer negotiation.

    Deliverable: two scored mocks with written notes and negotiation checklist.

  6. 06

    Thriving in your new role

    Once you start: first 30, 60, and 90 days, stakeholder mapping, and how to get noticed for the right reasons early — so the offer becomes a strong first chapter, not a scramble.

    Deliverable: 30/60/90 plan and a post-start check-in.

FAQ

Practical questions

What format are the sessions?

Live video, one-to-one. You will get a calendar link after booking. Written artefacts (roadmap, feedback, templates) are delivered between sessions.

How long does the programme take?

Eight weeks is the default cadence — roughly one session per stage, with room for application work between calls. We can compress or stretch slightly if your diary needs it.

What if I do not land a role?

There is no job guarantee. Coaching improves how you position, prepare, and decide — outcomes still depend on the market, your effort between sessions, and timing. I will be honest about fit and readiness; I will not promise an offer.

Can I reschedule a session?

Yes, with at least 48 hours’ notice where possible. Back-to-back late cancellations may mean we lose a slot in that week rather than stacking endless deferrals — the programme works best when momentum holds.
